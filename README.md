# The Hedonic Primacy Project

This repository hosts the website for **The Hedonic Primacy Project**, an independent research initiative by Thomas S. Peacock (University of Queensland) on hedonic ethics, the neuroscience of adaptation, and the engineering of conscious experience.

The site is served via GitHub Pages at:

**https://tomtopia007.github.io/**

## Contents

The site hosts two research papers:

1. **The Case for Hedonic Primacy: A Framework for Consciousness, Adaptation, and the Engineering of Experience** — a standalone philosophical paper defending hedonic primacy, consciousness-centric ethics, open individualism, and a precautionary account of cosmic-scale hedonic engineering.

2. **Hedonic Technology: The Neuroscience of Adaptation and the Computational Signatures of Peak Experience** — an empirical companion covering the multi-layer architecture of hedonic adaptation, computational work using the FlyWire Drosophila connectome and Meta's TRIBE v2 brain encoding model, and proposed experimental interventions. The neuroscience paper is preliminary and was produced with AI-assisted literature review and analysis; individual findings may require independent verification.

Both papers are available as PDF and DOCX downloads from the site.
